Who we are

We believe complex markets become accessible when business knowledge, local market expertise and reliable relationships come together.

Our purpose

Companies need direction and access to suitable business partners. Local trading networks have experience, relationships and trust that outsiders often find difficult to assess. URIM connects these two sides and makes sure that both benefit from working together.

We make hard-to-reach markets accessible and enable better collaboration between companies and local trading networks.

Who we work with

Our clients are corporate groups and international companies that manage their businesses across multiple countries or enter new emerging markets. We work with executive leadership, strategy, investment and commercial teams who need to understand connections, compare possible actions and explain the reasoning behind their decisions.

On the other side stand distributors, agents, traders and local networks. With URIM they gain access to business opportunities, find suitable partners and can demonstrate a reliability that has previously been difficult to make visible.

Clients think of URIM when they ask: how do we understand changes in our markets, make decisions about our business and find a reliable way to put them into practice on the ground?

Our mission

We connect business knowledge with global and local market intelligence to support decisions with a clear rationale, and develop the infrastructure to put them into practice on the ground.

Our differentiation lies in connecting company context, global and local information, reasoned recommendations and feedback from implementation. For clients this becomes a continuous process: identify a development, assess its significance, decide on an action and track the outcome.

How we work

Every core workflow shows what is changing, why it matters to the client, which action appears justified and what is needed to put it into practice. It then makes it possible to trace what actually happened.

03

Enable access

Learn from outcomes

What actually happened, traced back to the decision.

  • Recommendations require a rationale and a clear confidence level.Users can examine the underlying information.
  • Partner reliability is established through defined evidence.“Unknown”, “verified” and “proven through business performance” are distinct, traceable statuses.
  • Global and local information is connected within the company’s context.An international market indicator is assessed alongside the relevant conditions on the ground.

What we hold to

Clarity
We prioritise information and explain its significance for a specific decision.
Reliability
We make sources, freshness of information, uncertainty and the limits of our claims visible.
Respect for local context
We develop with existing traders, intermediaries and networks, taking their ways of working into account.
Mutual benefit
We state what each participant gains in return for their contribution.
Agency
We give participants clear, understandable control over the sharing of their business information.

Additional data sharing requires a clear purpose, control over what is shared, and a tangible benefit for the partner providing the information.
